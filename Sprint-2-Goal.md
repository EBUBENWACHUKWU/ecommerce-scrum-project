# Sprint 2 Goal

## Objective

Enhance the shopping experience by enabling customers to search for products, add products to a shopping cart, and complete purchases through checkout.

## Business Value

This sprint delivers the core purchasing workflow required for customers to successfully shop on the platform.

## Selected User Stories

* ECWP-7: Search Products
* ECWP-8: Add To Cart
* ECWP-9: Checkout
